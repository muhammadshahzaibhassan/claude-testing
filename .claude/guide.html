Never Do This

🚫Recolour gradient outside #F0647A → #BF1040
🚫Add drop shadows, glows, bevels, or emboss
🚫Outline or stroke any logo element
🚫Place logo inside a container shape (box/circle)
🚫Rearrange the icon and wordmark relationship
🚫Change the Orbitron typeface or letter-spacing
🚫Use on busy backgrounds without a solid panel
🚫Use raster files below 300 DPI for print
🚫Animate without brand-approved motion spec
04 — Brand Voice & Tone
Brand Voice & Tone
Every word Vertex publishes passes through three filters. If the copy doesn't feel Precise, Relentless, and Intelligent simultaneously — rewrite it.
Brand Personality
Word 01
Precise
Every decision is specific, tested, and defensible. We don't approximate. We build to exact specifications and deliver exactly what we said we would. No rounding up. No vague outcomes.
Word 02
Relentless
We don't stop at good enough. We pursue maximum efficiency without apology — bold, uncompromising, results-first energy in everything we publish and everything we build.
Word 03
Intelligent
AI-native thinking in every system we build. We anticipate problems before they surface. Our work is always one step ahead — analytical, strategic, built on evidence not intuition.

05 — Pattern System
Pattern & Texture Direction
The brand pattern extends the logo's rounded-square visual DNA into background surfaces. Used at low opacity it creates brand-coded texture without introducing a competing visual language. Barely there. Unmistakably Vertex.
Motif & Variants
Light Version
#BF1040 · 8–12% opacity · on #F8F9FA
Dark Version
#F0647A · 8% opacity · on #0A0A0B
Accent Blue Version
#0A84FF · 6% opacity · tech/data contexts
✓ Approved Uses
✅Social media story / reel backgrounds
✅Presentation slide backgrounds at 8–10% opacity
✅Email newsletter footer bands
✅Business card reverse side at 15% opacity
✅Packaging interior / tissue paper
✅Apparel all-over print at 30–40% opacity
✗ Not Approved
🚫Behind any logo placement — visual conflict
🚫Full opacity in any context whatsoever
🚫Any layout where it competes with messaging
🚫Hero sections where headline legibility suffers
🚫Minimum 60% background must always be visible

Asset Naming Convention
Format: [asset-type]-[variant]-[color-mode]-[orientation][@scale].[ext] ✅ logo-primary-color-horizontal@2x.png ✅ logo-reversed-white-stacked.svg ✅ logo-icon-color.svg ✅ pattern-brand-dark@2x.png ✅ favicon-32x32.png Rules: — Always lowercase · hyphen-separated · no spaces · no underscores — Always include color mode: color / white / black / grayscale — Always suffix @2x / @3x on raster screen files — SVG files never need @scale (infinitely scalable) — Never use: final · v2 · new · updated · date stamps in filenames
