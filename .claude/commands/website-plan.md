# Vertex Automations - Content Brief

Use this as the writing brief for all website pages. The public site exists in two surfaces: Flask templates in `templates/` and mirrored static pages in `frontend/public/`. For pages that exist in both, the message should stay consistent.

## Core Marketing Pages

- Audience: Business owners, operations leaders, founders, department heads.

## Notes for the writer
- Keep language practical, confident, and business-focused.
- Emphasize AI automation, workflow orchestration, operational efficiency, and measurable outcomes.
- Avoid vague marketing claims without a concrete business result.
- Use consistent terminology across pages: automation systems, workflows, AI agents, integrations, ROI, governance, and human review.
- For mirrored pages, match the same core message even if the visual layout differs.
